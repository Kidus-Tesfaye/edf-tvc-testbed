What this case demonstrates

The same single EDF and four vanes climbed to 5 m, hovered for eight seconds, then landed using ArduPilot's native LAND mode. Maximum sideways distance was 0.387 m. First touchdown was 0.267 m from launch at 0.273 m/s downward. It settled upright and automatically disarmed 3.63 s after first contact.

This is a modest steady-wind example, not the worst case or an outdoor flight prediction. Across all eight scenarios the largest distance was 0.597 m; all eight landed. One other case bounced 8.6 mm before settling. Actual GPS accuracy, printed-leg response and EDF airflow near the ground remain unvalidated.

How to read the 3D views

The left view uses true distances; the ground circle has a 1 m radius around the original launch point. The height mast marks 1 m intervals, with the upper ring at 5 m above the initial CG height. The blue trace is the recorded CG path. Ground height and CAD placement use the model's floor and CG offsets.

The right view is a close-up that follows the vehicle. It does not exaggerate tilt or vane deflections. Body attitude and four vane angles come from the recorded states; rigid parts do not flex. CAD axes X/Y are transverse tilt axes and Z is the vertical/axial axis. This is a 30 Hz display of a 1200 Hz state recording, interpolated for viewing; use the raw recording for impact loads and very short events.

Run the simulation yourselves

Watching this playback needs only a WebGL-capable browser and works offline after extraction. Rerunning the controller/physics requires Windows x64, licensed MATLAB R2025b + Simulink, and Python 3.12. Open README.md for setup and the single command to launch the representative case. No Pixhawk, EDF, serial port or additional controller is required.
